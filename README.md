## What Changes at Each Breakpoint

- Phones: Header text shrinks. Menu cards are stacked. Visit Us cards are stacked. All are in a single column.
- From md (768px): Header text is enlarged. Menu cards are still stacked, but in two columns instead of one. Visit Us cards are also in two columns.
- From lg (992px): Header text is still enlarged. Menu cards are stacked, but are now in three columns. Visit Us cards remain in two columns.
